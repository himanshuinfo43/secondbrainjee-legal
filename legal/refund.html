01

Overview

This Refund Policy governs purchases made within the Second Brain for JEE Android app. All payments are processed securely through Razorpay, a licensed Payment Aggregator regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

We offer refunds in specific circumstances described below. This policy is intended to be fair to genuine users while protecting against abuse.

02

Payment Processing

Payments are handled by Razorpay Software Private Limited, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We never store your full card number, CVV, or banking credentials on our servers.

  • Payments may be made via UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and wallets.
  • All transactions are in Indian Rupees (INR).
  • You will receive a payment confirmation email from Razorpay upon successful payment.
  • All subscription prices are inclusive of applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) at the prevailing rate (currently 18% for digital services). The GST-inclusive price is what you see at checkout — there are no additional charges.
Save your Razorpay Order ID (included in your payment confirmation email). You'll need it when requesting a refund.
03

Refund Eligibility

✓ Eligible for Refund
  • Duplicate payment charged for the same subscription period
  • Charged after verified cancellation
  • Technical failure prevented access to premium features for 72+ hours
  • Incorrect amount charged due to a pricing error on our end
  • Subscription activated but premium features completely inaccessible
✕ Not Eligible for Refund
  • Change of mind after accessing premium features
  • Forgetting to cancel before auto-renewal
  • Dissatisfaction with AI response quality
  • Account suspended for Terms violations
  • Partial period refunds (mid-cycle cancellations)
  • Requests made after 7 days from charge date
Time Limit: Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the disputed charge date. Requests submitted after this window will not be processed, except in cases of verified billing errors.
04

Non-Refundable Cases

The following situations are explicitly excluded from our refund policy:

  • Auto-renewal charges where the user failed to cancel prior to the renewal date.
  • Partial-period cancellations — if you cancel mid-cycle, you retain access until end of period but are not refunded for unused days.
  • Free trial conversions where the trial terms were clearly disclosed at sign-up.
  • Dissatisfaction with AI accuracy — AI limitations are disclosed in our Terms.
  • Account termination for cause (Terms violations, abuse, fraud).
  • Purchases made on behalf of someone else without their consent — report such cases to your bank.

06

Refund Timeline

  • Review: We review your request within 2 business days of receiving it.
  • Decision: We'll email you our decision (approved/rejected) with reasoning.
  • Processing: Approved refunds are initiated to Razorpay within 1–2 business days of approval.
  • Credit to account: Depending on your bank and payment method, you'll see the credit within 5–10 business days of initiation.

UPI refunds are typically faster (1–3 days). Card refunds may take up to 10 business days. We have no control over bank processing times after initiating the refund.

07

Subscription Cancellation

How to Cancel

  • Open the app → Settings → Account → Subscription → Cancel Plan
  • Alternatively, if subscribed via Google Play: Visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions and cancel from there.
  • You'll receive a cancellation confirmation email.
Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid being charged for the next period. Cancelling after the renewal date does not reverse the charge for that period.

After Cancellation

  • You retain premium access until the end of the paid period.
  • Your account reverts to the free plan at period end.
  • Your data is preserved and not deleted upon cancellation.

Failed Payments

If your auto-renewal payment fails, we will notify you via email and in-app alert. You will have a 7-day grace period to update your payment method before premium access is suspended. No refund is applicable for the grace period.

08

Abuse Prevention & Non-Abuse Clause

Our refund policy exists to protect genuine users. We actively monitor refund patterns to prevent abuse.

  • Subscribing, using premium features, and then requesting a refund as a routine practice is considered abuse.
  • Requesting multiple refunds across billing cycles will result in account review and potential suspension.
  • Providing false or misleading information in a refund request is grounds for permanent account ban.
Pattern Detection: We reserve the right to decline refund requests from accounts that show a pattern of subscribe-use-refund behavior, even if individual requests appear legitimate.

If your account is flagged for refund abuse, you will be notified and given an opportunity to respond before any action is taken.

09

Disputes & Chargebacks

We strongly encourage you to contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank or card issuer. Chargebacks are costly and time-consuming for both parties.

  • Contact us first at support.secondbrainjee@gmail.com — we respond within 48 hours.
  • If we cannot resolve your concern, you may escalate to Razorpay's grievance process or your bank.
  • If your concern remains unresolved after contacting us, you may raise a grievance directly with Razorpay at razorpay.com/support or via the RBI's CPGRAMS portal for payment-related disputes.
Chargeback consequences: Filing a chargeback without first contacting us may result in immediate account suspension pending investigation. Fraudulent chargebacks may be reported to relevant authorities and Razorpay's fraud prevention systems.

For unauthorized payment claims (someone else used your payment method without permission), please contact both your bank and us simultaneously.
