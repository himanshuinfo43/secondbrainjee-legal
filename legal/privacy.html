Who We Are

Second Brain for JEE ("we," "our," or "us") is an AI-powered study platform built for JEE aspirants. We operate the Second Brain for JEE Android application. Our registered contact is support.secondbrainjee@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read it carefully.

03

Authentication

We use Supabase Authentication to manage sign-in and account security. You may sign in via:

  • Email and password (credentials stored encrypted in Supabase)
  • Google OAuth (we receive only your public Google profile: name, email, profile picture)

We do not store raw passwords. All authentication tokens are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest.

Session management: Auth sessions expire automatically. You can sign out from all devices via account settings, which invalidates all active tokens immediately.
04

Planner Uploads

You may import study planners (PDF, images, or structured data) into the app. When you do:

  • Files are uploaded securely to Supabase Storage using your authenticated session.
  • Files are associated only with your account and are not shared with other users.
  • Uploaded files may be sent to Gemini AI for parsing and extraction — only as needed to fulfill your request.
  • Files are stored only for as long as your account exists, or until you delete them manually.
Important: Do not upload documents containing sensitive personal information (Aadhaar, PAN, bank details) as planner content. Upload study-related materials only.
05

AI Chat Usage

The AI Chat feature is powered by Google Gemini, accessed via Supabase Edge Functions. Here is exactly what happens when you send a message:

  • Your message is transmitted over TLS to our Supabase Edge Function.
  • The Edge Function forwards it to Google's Gemini API for inference.
  • The AI response is returned and stored in your chat history in Supabase.
  • Your conversation history is tied to your account and not shared publicly.

Google may process your messages per their Privacy Policy and Gemini API terms. We recommend not sharing personally identifying or highly sensitive information in AI chat.

AI Limitations Disclosure

AI responses are generated automatically and may be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated. Do not rely solely on AI output for critical exam preparation decisions. Always cross-verify with authoritative study materials.

06

Analytics

We collect anonymized usage analytics to improve the app. This includes:

  • Feature usage frequency and session length
  • App crash and error reports
  • Device type, OS version, and app version
  • General geographic region (country/state level only)

Analytics data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you individually. We do not use third-party ad networks or sell analytics data.

07

Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to send study reminders, mission alerts, and Night Audit notifications.

  • Notifications are only sent after you grant permission on your device.
  • Your FCM token is stored in our database and linked to your account.
  • You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or in-app settings.
  • Disabling notifications does not affect app functionality.

Children's Privacy

If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under 13 without consent, we will delete it promptly. Contact us at support.secondbrainjee@gmail.com.
